# Voyage 4

Voyage 4 embeds the shared 768-token chunks through OpenRouter and retrieves 1,024-dimensional vectors with exact cosine search in Milvus before collapsing hits to whole documents.

Together with Voyage 4 Large and Voyage 4 Lite, this Solution exposes the quality point in the middle of the current Voyage family. Embeddings are stored in a durable content-addressed cache.
