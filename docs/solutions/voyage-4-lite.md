# Voyage 4 Lite

Voyage 4 Lite embeds the shared 768-token chunks through OpenRouter and retrieves 1,024-dimensional vectors with exact cosine search in Milvus before collapsing hits to whole documents.

It supplies the low-cost endpoint of the Voyage 4 family comparison while preserving the same corpus, chunking, vector index, and metrics. Embeddings are stored in a durable content-addressed cache.
