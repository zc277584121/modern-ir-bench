# BM25 · default

The full-document and chunked BM25 Solutions use Milvus built-in BM25 with an
English analyzer for English pools, a Chinese analyzer for Chinese pools, and
the standard `k1=1.2, b=0.75` scoring parameters. The Task keeps the two
language candidate pools independent.

The chunked variant uses the benchmark-wide pinned chunk boundaries: a target
of 768 BGE-M3-tokenizer tokens, a 384–896 token range, 128-token overlap, and
paragraph-boundary preference. Chunk hits are collapsed to document rankings.

These two Solutions isolate the effect of indexing the complete document or
the benchmark's shared chunks. They do not perform query expansion, semantic
encoding, reranking, or score fusion.
